FAQ

Questions before you ride

What do I need to bring?

An ID document for the deposit hold. Helmet, lock and phone holder are provided.

Is there a deposit?

Yes: we place a temporary hold of €150 (€500 for e-bikes and cargo bikes) on a bank card, released when you return the bike.

Can children ride with me?

Yes. We have kids bikes from age 4, child seats, trailers and cargo bikes for the little ones.

What happens if it rains?

You can move your booking to another day free of charge, or borrow one of our rain ponchos.

Can I return the bike later than planned?

Yes, extra time is charged at the hourly rate. Just call us so we can keep the bike for you.
